Why two iterations? The pipeline caught its own failure

Per-gene two-way ANOVA on the naive pass showed cell-line identity explaining more variance than the biology of interest. Instead of reporting confounded results, the loop flagged it and re-ran with batch-aware statistics.

Mechanistic context

Pathway enrichment (GSEA, Hallmark sets) and transcription-factor regulon activity provide the "why" behind the ranking — targets sitting in coherent senescence programs are more credible than isolated hits.

Canonical senescence programs appear where expected: interferon and inflammatory (SASP-adjacent) signaling enriched upward, proliferation programs (E2F targets) down — with TP53 and E2F4 regulons among the strongest signals.

Validation against known biology

20 literature senescence markers and senolytic targets act as positive controls. A marker counts as recovered when the pipeline finds it changed in the expected direction. This is what makes the demo falsifiable.

– recovered in iteration 1

Including senolytic drug targets BCL2L1 (BCL-xL, navitoclax) and MDM2 (p53 axis) — both recovered with the expected direction. Batch correction fixed markers the naive pass missed, e.g. TP53.

Does it generalize?

Leave-one-cell-line-out: a classifier is trained on four cell lines and asked to label the held-out fifth. Honest reporting — performance varies by cell line, which is exactly the kind of caveat a reviewer should see.
